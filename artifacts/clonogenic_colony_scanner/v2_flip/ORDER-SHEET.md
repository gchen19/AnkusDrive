## Cart 1 — printed parts, Craftcloud (~$85 for rev 2.1's five parts; the quotes were taken on the three rev 2.0 parts at $77)

1. Go to https://craftcloud3d.com/en/upload and drop in the five files from `v21/`:
   `frame.stl`, `tower.stl`, `pi_sled.stl`, `cam_cover.stl`, `door.stl` (units: millimetres).
2. Confirm settings (United States, USD, mm) → "See Materials & Prices".
3. Search **PETG** → Select material → finish **Standard**, infill **20 %** → colour **Black** →
   See offers.
4. Pick **Corvallis3D, Oregon — $76.86** (7–10 d production, UPS), or the same vendor's
   **$94.48** express row if you want it by ~Sep 23.
5. Order notes: *"tower.stl: print upside down (flat camera plate on the bed), no supports.
   frame.stl: floor ring down. door.stl: flat. 3 walls, 0.2 mm layers, matte black if you have it."*
6. Sign in / create the account at checkout and pay. (Anonymous uploads are not shareable, so I
   could not hand you a pre-filled cart link; steps 1–4 take about a minute.)
