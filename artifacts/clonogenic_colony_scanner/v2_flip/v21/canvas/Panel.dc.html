Clonogenic plate scanner · variant B · rev 3.0
Ceiling, wiring and the front
What the user sees
A 2-inch 320 × 240 IPS display behind a window in the front wall and a 16 mm stainless pushbutton on the roof beside the camera. The screen says what to do next in plain words (LID UP · A1 REAR-LEFT · PRESS, then FLIP TOWARD YOU), then shows the six well counts and a thumbnail of the plate. The bar across the top of the screen is the same state in colour: green ready, white capturing, blinking amber flip, red problem.
How it connects
The light pad's USB lead plugs into a Pi USB port, so one 27 W supply powers everything.
