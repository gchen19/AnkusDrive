One rule
Tap = go. Hold = no. While you hold, an orange bar fills across the top of the footer; let go early and nothing happens. The footer always names both actions for the current state, and the screen's colour bar shows the state in colour from across the bench. Stand at the cassette face; the loading slot is on your left.
When things go wrong
Walk away at FLIP and after five minutes the label photo is filed under _incomplete and the scanner returns to READY. Reject a plate after seeing its counts and it moves to _rejected, still on disk. Every step is written to the run's status file the moment it starts, so a folder never claims more than happened; at boot, any run left in progress by a power cut is moved to _incomplete and announced on screen once.
