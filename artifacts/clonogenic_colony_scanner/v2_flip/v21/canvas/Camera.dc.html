How the camera fits
The tower's top plate carries four bosses on the HQ camera's 30 × 30 mm pattern and a Ø34 hole. The 8 mm CS lens screws into the board and hangs down through the hole into the dark tube; the board sits on 8 mm standoffs so its tripod block clears the plate. The cover drops onto four pegs, its rear slot passing the ribbon toward the Pi bay.
